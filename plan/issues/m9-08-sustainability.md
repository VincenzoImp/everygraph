**Why.** The largest non-technical risk is a single maintainer on a multi-year project. Comparable projects died or stalled from exactly this, or from single-company control.

**Scope**
- [ ] Funding applications, with status as of the 2026-11 research (re-verify every date before applying): Sovereign Tech Fund (rolling, infrastructure-focused, €50k–1.3M); NLnet programmes (the NGI Zero Commons final call closed 2026-06; other calls listed for early 2027); NumFOCUS small development grants (≤ $10k); ERC Proof of Concept (~€150k) and national/Horizon calls through a university
- [ ] Academic partnerships: groups working on graph compression and large-graph algorithms (the webgraph-rs authors are in Italy), computational social science labs, the GraphSense team
- [ ] Governance document: decision process, maintainer ladder, release rights for at least two people, trademark ownership
- [ ] Path to a neutral home after traction (NumFOCUS, OpenJS for the viewer, or a small foundation)
- [ ] Business-model options that keep the core permissive (support, hosted map publishing, enterprise integration) — and the acquisition risk such a company carries for users

**Acceptance**
- At least two funding applications submitted and a second maintainer with release rights
