1 · Actions

Buttons do something; links go somewhere. A view has one primary button, and it names the thing it does.

Dialogs

Use a dialog only when a decision is needed before anything else can happen. Rule: the buttons repeat the action in words (“Delete transfers.eg”, never “OK”); a destructive dialog says what is lost and what is kept.

confirm

Find communities again?

This replaces the 6 levels of the map with new ones. Links to regions of the current map will stop working.

It takes about 2 min 10 s and stays inside the 8 GB budget.

destructive confirm

Delete transfers.eg?

The graph file (9.3 GB) and its 2 saved maps will be removed from this machine. This cannot be undone.

The source file transfers.parquet is not touched; building the graph again takes about 3 min 30 s.

share a link to the view

Position and zoom — always included Selection — Lending Colour and time window

Toast

Use to confirm something that just happened and needs no decision; it leaves by itself after 6 s, or stays if it reports a failure. Rule: one line, an icon, and at most one action; never the only place an error is shown.
